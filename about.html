OUR TRADITION SINCE 2016

Rooted in Earth, Perfected by Smoke

In Ponnani, Kerala, we honor the ancient Arabian craft of slow-cooking meat inside sealed underground clay pits over seasoned hardwood embers.

KUZHI MANDI PONNANI, KERALA

Flavor You Simply Cannot Rush

Al Mashi Mandi was founded with a single, uncompromising standard: never cut corners on time. True Kuzhi Mandi is not merely steamed or baked in an oven — it is born underground.

Every morning at dawn, our pit masters ignite seasoned hardwood inside deep clay-lined earth pits. When the wood reduces to glowing, fragrant embers, whole spiced cuts of young baby mutton and farm-fresh chicken are suspended inside. The pit is sealed airtight with clay and heavy iron covers.

For four patient hours, the trapped heat and wood-smoke permeate every fiber of meat until it becomes fork-tender, dripping its spiced essence directly into the steaming basmati rice below.

THE METHOD

The 4 Pillars of Al Mashi

01

Natural Hardwood Embers

We never use gas burners or electric heating. Real charcoal and wood embers create the authentic deep aroma of Arabian pit roasting.

02

18-Spice Secret Hawayej

Freshly ground green cardamom, black lime (loomi), cloves, cinnamon bark, and saffron ground by hand every morning.

03

Airtight Sealed Pit

The kuzhi pit is sealed completely with clay mud, preserving 100% of moisture and infusing the meat's natural juices into the rice.

04

Fresh Daily Portions

Zero leftovers. Every grain of rice and cut of meat is prepared fresh for lunch and dinner services until the pit runs out.
