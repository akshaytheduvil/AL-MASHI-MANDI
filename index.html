ABOUT AL MASHI

Rooted in Tradition, Served with Pride

Al Mashi Mandi was born from a simple belief — that great Mandi cannot be rushed. We cook our meat in a traditional underground pit (kuzhi), sealed and fired by natural wood embers for hours until the flavor deepens and the meat falls off the bone.

Every plate we serve carries the aroma of smoke, the warmth of Arabian spices, and the hospitality of a family that treats every guest like their own.
